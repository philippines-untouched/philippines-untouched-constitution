# Glossary

**Version:** 1.0 — Ratified
**Status:** Ratified by the authoring foundation upon completion of all fifty chapters of the Constitution, Version 1.0.

---

## How to Use This Glossary

- Terms are listed alphabetically.
- Definitions are constitutional — they define how the term is used in this Manual, not necessarily its general or legal meaning.
- A term marked with an asterisk (*) has its own substantive chapter in the Constitution.
- This glossary is the authoritative source for defined terms. Once a term is defined here, it shall be used consistently throughout the Constitution.

---

## A

**Amendment**
A formal, documented change to the Constitution. Amendments are recorded in the Revision History (0.3) and the Decision Log. The Constitution evolves only by formal amendment; no version is destroyed or concealed.

**Appendix**
A supplementary section at the end of the Constitution containing reference material (Glossary, Document Hierarchy, Cross-Reference, Referenced Standards). Appendices are part of the Constitution but contain supporting information rather than constitutional principles.

**Articles of Incorporation**
The legal instrument that creates the Company as a juristic entity under Philippine law. Subordinate to the Constitution.

**Assurance & Audit**
The Company's practice of verification, auditing and continual assessment, by which its standards are made real and provable. *

**Authoring Foundation**
The body of ratified framework documents (Authoring Principles, Table of Contents, Style Guide, Decision Log) that governs the creation and amendment of the Constitution. Established before any substantive chapter is written.

**Authoring Principles**
The ratified framework document (`authoring/00-authoring-principles.md`) that establishes the rules by which the Constitution is written, revised and ratified. Binding on every future author and amendment.

**Automation**
The use of systems to perform tasks reliably and efficiently. The Company embraces automation where it serves its purpose, and keeps human judgement where judgement matters. *

---

## B

**Board**
The Board of Directors of Philippines Untouched OPC, elected or appointed in accordance with the Company's Articles of Incorporation and By-Laws.

**Brand Architecture**
The structured relationship between the Company's corporate identity (Philippines Untouched), its principal consumer brand (PUM), and any future product brands or sub-brands. Every brand relates to the Company directly; no brand is subordinate to another. *

**By-Laws**
The rules governing the internal management of the Company. Subordinate to the Constitution.

---

## C

**Capital Allocation**
The disciplined investment of the Company's financial resources for long-term benefit, in service of the Company's purpose. *

**Centre of Excellence**
The Company's aspiration to be recognised as a Centre of Excellence for sustainable Philippine agriculture — a place where students, farmers, researchers and industry professionals can learn, observe and contribute. A strategic objective, not an achieved status.

**Certification**
Formal recognition by an independent body that the Company's systems meet a recognised standard. Certification is a strategic objective of the Company and shall never be implied as achieved until formally obtained.

**Chapter**
A numbered section of the Constitution, organised within a Part. Each chapter addresses one meaningful subject and satisfies the Definition of Done before it is considered complete.

**Climate Resilience**
The Company's preparation for environmental change, strengthening the land and the Company to protect long-term productivity. *

**Community Prosperity**
The Company's commitment to strengthening surrounding communities through employment, education and opportunity. The goal is shared prosperity, not charity; capability, not dependency. *

**Company**
Philippines Untouched OPC, the Corporation governed by this Constitution. Always capitalised in the constitutional text.

**Constancy of Purpose**
The principle that the Company's founding principles shall remain constant while its strategies, technologies and operations evolve to meet the needs of each generation. *

**Constitution**
The Corporate Governance & Strategy Manual of Philippines Untouched OPC — the highest governing document of the Company. All subordinate documents derive their authority from this document. The terms "Constitution" and "Manual" are used interchangeably in this text.

**Constitutional Review**
The deliberate process by which the Constitution evolves through formal amendment while preserving its founding principles. *

**Continuous Improvement**
The Company's commitment to incremental improvement throughout every part of the organisation, one meaningful step at a time. *

**Core Values**
The enduring values expected of every person acting on behalf of the Company: Integrity, Quality, Stewardship, Trust, Excellence. *

**Corporate Reputation**
The accumulated judgement of everyone who has dealt with the Company — one of the Company's most valuable long-term assets, earned through conduct and protected with care. *

**Customer Trust**
The foundation of the Company's relationship with its customers — earned through quality and honesty, maintained through consistency, and protected above short-term gain. *

---

## D

**Decision Framework**
The structured process by which significant business decisions are evaluated against the Company's principles. *

**Decision Log**
The permanent record of significant authoring decisions and their reasons (`authoring/04-decision-log.md`). Part of the authoring framework, not the Constitution itself.

**Definition of Done**
The eleven acceptance criteria that every chapter must satisfy before it is considered complete (Authoring Principles, Section 8).

**Digital Transformation**
The use of digital systems to support the Company's decisions and work — informing decisions without replacing human judgement. *

**Document Hierarchy**
The structured relationship showing how every policy, standard, manual and procedure derives authority from the Constitution. (See Appendix B.)

---

## E

**Environmental Responsibility**
The Company's commitment to protect biodiversity, natural resources and ecological health. *

**Ethical Leadership**
The standards of integrity expected from all leaders of the Company. Leadership is stewardship. *

**Evidence**
Trustworthy, verifiable information that supports claims, decisions and institutional memory. The Constitution prefers evidence over unsupported assertion.

**Excellence Without Comparison**
The principle that the Company strives to be the best it can be through its own standards, not by reference to competitors. *

---

## F

**Financial Philosophy**
The principle that money is a tool for achieving the Company's purpose rather than its purpose. *

**Food Safety**
A non-negotiable responsibility of the Company — the safety of everything the Company produces, at every stage from cultivation to delivery. *

**Founder**
The individual who conceived and established the Company. Referenced in historical context and the Founder's Foreword only. The Constitution itself is not about the Founder.

**Founder's Foreword**
The Front Matter section (0.5) in which the Founder speaks in personal voice, explaining the origins and experiences that led to the Company's founding. The one place where personal voice is permitted.

**Founding Principles**
The thirteen non-negotiable, enduring principles from which every corporate decision flows. Established in Chapter 5 and cross-referenced throughout the Constitution. *

**Front Matter**
The preliminary sections of the Constitution (0.1–0.6) that precede Part I. Includes Cover Page, Document Control, Revision History, Dedication, Founder's Foreword, and How to Use This Manual.

**Future Leader Test**
The standard against which every chapter is measured: a future leader who has never met the Founder must be able to read the Constitution and understand what the Company is, what it stands for, and what has been entrusted to them (Authoring Principles, Section 7).

---

## G

**GAP**
The Company's software platform and event-sourced systems for Good Agricultural Practices. A technology implementation within the broader principle of evidence-based decision making.

**GLOBAL G.A.P.**
The internationally recognised standard for Good Agricultural Practices. The Company's strategic objective is certification under GLOBAL G.A.P. Integrated Farm Assurance (IFA) Smart Version 6. *

**Glossary**
The constitutional reference of defined terms. This appendix (A) is the authoritative source for terminology used in the Constitution.

**Good Agricultural Practice**
The disciplined application of knowledge to farming — understanding the land, managing inputs responsibly, protecting food safety, preserving the environment and caring for workers, with documented processes and verifiable evidence.

**Governance Framework**
The structure through which authority, accountability and responsibility are exercised throughout the Company. No individual is above the Company's principles. *

**Growth Strategy**
Sustainable expansion — growth that strengthens the Company without compromising its principles, its quality or its stewardship. *

## I

**Information Integrity**
The principle that information is a strategic asset requiring accuracy, security and permanence. *

**Innovation Strategy**
The Company's approach to innovation — solving meaningful problems rather than creating unnecessary complexity. *

**Institutional Knowledge**
The collective expertise, records and understanding accumulated by the Company over time. A strategic asset that must be preserved through organisational memory. *

**International English**
The spelling and language convention used throughout the Constitution, following Commonwealth usage (e.g., organisation, recognised, prioritise).

**International Strategy**
The Company's export aspirations and its commitment to meeting internationally recognised standards. *

---

## K

**Knowledge & Education**
The Company's commitment to continual learning, agricultural education and knowledge sharing. *

---

## L

**Legacy**
The enduring value the Company leaves for future generations — including financial sustainability, meaningful employment, strong governance, excellent products, healthy land, institutional knowledge, and a trusted Philippine brand. Each generation is a steward rather than merely an owner. *

**Long-Term Strategy**
The Company's long-term direction, measured in decades rather than years. Its principles remain constant while its strategies evolve. *

## M

**Manual**
See **Constitution**.

**Manufacturing Philosophy**
The principles governing food production and value-added manufacturing — the continuation of the Company's agriculture, carrying its standards from field to customer. *

**Market Strategy**
How the Company competes — through quality, trust and authenticity, in the premium segment rather than primarily on price. *

**Mission**
What the Company does every day to achieve its Vision. *

---

## N

**North Star**
The guiding question of the entire authoring project: *"What kind of organisation should Philippines Untouched be if it is still operating successfully after the founder is gone?"*

## O

**One Bite at a Time**
The working principle that large problems are addressed one meaningful part at a time. Applied to the authoring process: chapters are developed incrementally, not all at once.

**OPC**
One Person Corporation — the legal structure of Philippines Untouched under Philippine corporation law.

**Operational Excellence**
The principles underpinning efficient, consistent and scalable operations — the Company's principles made visible in its daily work. *

**Organisational Design**
The design of the Company as an integrated system rather than a collection of independent departments. *

**Organisational Memory**
The Company's capability to capture, preserve and transmit knowledge across generations. The keystone chapter (41) of the Constitution. *

---

## P

**Part**
A major division of the Constitution, grouping related chapters under a unifying theme. The Constitution has eleven Parts (I–XI).

**People Grow Prosperity**
The principle that investing in people creates enduring business success, and that those who contribute should share appropriately in the value they create. *

**Philippines Untouched**
The trading name and corporate identity of Philippines Untouched OPC. The name under which the Company conducts its business.

**PUM**
The Company's principal consumer and retail brand. The name by which customers encounter the Company.

**Purpose**
The Company's reason for existing — beyond financial success. *

---

## Q

**Quality Before Profit**
The principle that quality and integrity take precedence over short-term financial gain. A central, non-negotiable commitment of the Company. *

**Quality Management**
The deliberate management of quality as the Company's primary competitive advantage — defined standards, documented processes, verification and continual review. *

---

## R

**Reciprocal Responsibility**
The mutual obligation between the Company and its people: the Company shall not expect a level of commitment from its people that it is unwilling to honour with fairness, respect and integrity.

**Resource Stewardship**
The responsible management of land, water, energy and materials — resources entrusted to the Company, to be preserved for future generations. *

**Revision History**
The permanent record of amendments made to the Constitution (0.3). No previous version is destroyed or concealed.

**Risk Governance**
How risk is understood, managed and balanced against opportunity — with no risk accepted that would compromise the Company's integrity or its future. *

---

## S

**Sole Shareholder**
The single owner of Philippines Untouched OPC, as permitted by Philippine OPC law. The Sole Shareholder is distinct from the Board and from management.

**Stewardship**
The principle that each generation is entrusted with the Company, the land, the people and the knowledge, and must leave them stronger for those who follow. *

**Style Guide**
The ratified framework document (`authoring/02-style-guide.md`) establishing the language, voice and conventions for all constitutional text.

**Subordinate Document**
Any document that derives its authority from the Constitution — including Articles of Incorporation, By-Laws, policies, strategies, plans, manuals, procedures, quality systems, and standards.

**Succession**
The orderly transfer of leadership and stewardship responsibility — the transfer of stewardship, not merely ownership. *

**Supply Chain Philosophy**
How suppliers and logistics partners become extensions of the Company's values, from sourcing to delivery. *

**Sustainable Development**
The Company's commitment to balancing environmental, social and economic outcomes. *

**Sustainable Profitability**
Profit as the consequence of creating enduring value — earned sustainably, never at the cost of the Company's principles or its future. *

---

## T

**Table of Contents**
The ratified chapter structure for the Constitution (`authoring/01-table-of-contents.md`). Defines the organisation of the Constitution into Parts, Chapters, Front Matter, and Appendices.

**Technology Philosophy**
The principle that technology is an enabler of quality, efficiency and organisational learning — adopted for the value it creates, never as an end in itself. *

**Traceability**
The capability to track a product's lifecycle from seed or planting material through cultivation, harvest, processing, packaging, storage, distribution and customer. *

**Trust Through Evidence**
The principle that accurate information, traceability and evidence-based decisions are the foundations of trust. *

**Vision**
What the Company ultimately seeks to become. *
