- **Part I — Identity:** *Who are we?*

- **Part V — Operations:** *How do we work?*

- **Part VII — Brand:** *How do we present ourselves?*
- **Part VIII — Technology:** *How do we enable?*
- **Part IX — Sustainability:** *How do we endure?*
- **Part X — Financial Stewardship:** *How do we sustain?*
- **Part XI — Legacy:** *What do we leave behind?*

## Author's Note — The Keystone Chapter

Of the fifty chapters in this edition, one deserves particular attention. It is not **Quality**, **GLOBAL G.A.P.**, or even **Stewardship**. It is **Chapter 41 — Organisational Memory**. This chapter ties together documentation, education, traceability, technology, governance and succession into a single idea: that the Company should never lose the knowledge it works so hard to acquire. In many ways, this Manual itself becomes the first and most enduring piece of that organisational memory.
