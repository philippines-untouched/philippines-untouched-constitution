# Philippines Untouched Constitution

**Corporate Governance & Strategy Manual of Philippines Untouched OPC**

This repository contains the constitutional governing document of **Philippines Untouched OPC** — an agricultural and food manufacturing enterprise established in the Philippines.

The principal consumer brand is **PUM**.

---

## Purpose

This repository holds the authoritative source for the Corporate Governance & Strategy Manual — the highest governing document of the Company. Every policy, business plan, operational manual, quality system and corporate decision derives its authority from the principles established within these chapters.

The Manual is written to remain relevant for decades and generations, and to survive changes in leadership, ownership, technology and markets.

### Authoring Framework

The authoring framework documents establish the rules, structure and voice by which the Constitution is written and amended. These are ratified and binding.

| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Authoring Principles | Rules for writing, revising and ratifying the Constitution |
| Table of Contents | Definitive 50-chapter structure across 11 Parts |
| Style Guide | Language, voice, "shall" usage, capitalisation, International English |
| Glossary | Defined terms (working draft; finalised after all chapters reach v1) |
| Decision Log | Permanent record of significant authoring decisions and their reasons |

### The Constitution

The Constitution itself lives in the `constitution/` directory. It is organised into eleven Parts, each answering a distinct question:

| Part | Theme | Question |
|---|---|---|
| I | Identity | Who are we? |
| II | Philosophy | What do we believe? |
| III | Governance | How do we make decisions? |
| IV | Strategy | Where are we going? |
| V | Operations | How do we work? |
| VI | Quality | How do we assure? |
| VII | Brand | How do we present ourselves? |
| VIII | Technology | How do we enable? |
| IX | Sustainability | How do we endure? |
| X | Financial Stewardship | How do we sustain? |
| XI | Legacy | What do we leave behind? |

The Front Matter (sections 0.1–0.6) precedes Part I and includes the Cover Page, Document Control, Revision History, Dedication, Founder's Foreword, and guidance on using the Manual.
