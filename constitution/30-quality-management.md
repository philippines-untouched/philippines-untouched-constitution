# Chapter 30 — Quality Management

*This chapter defines quality as the Company's primary competitive advantage. The Company's quality is not one department's responsibility; it is the standard of the whole Company, managed deliberately and proven by evidence.*

---

## 30.1 Quality Is the Company's Advantage

The Company's primary competitive advantage is its quality.

The Company competes in the premium segment through the excellence of its products and the trust it earns. That position rests upon one foundation: quality, delivered consistently and verifiably. The Company's quality is therefore not a supporting function — it is the basis of the Company's existence in the market.

---

## 30.2 Quality Is Everyone's Responsibility

Quality is the responsibility of every part of the Company and every person who acts on its behalf.

Quality is not created by an inspection at the end of the line. It is built into every stage of the Company's work — from the choice of seed to the moment of delivery — by the people who perform that work to the Company's standard.

The Company shall ensure that every person understands the standard expected of their work and is supported in meeting it.

---

## 30.3 The Quality System

The Company shall manage quality through a deliberate, documented and verifiable system.

The Company's quality management shall include:

- **Defined standards** — clear statements of what the Company's quality means for each product and process;
- **Documented processes** — the way work shall be performed, understood by all who perform it;
- **Verification** — checking that the standard was met, with evidence;
- **Traceability** — the ability to follow any product through its history;
- **Continual review** — examining results and improving the system over time;
- **Accountability** — clear responsibility for the quality of every part of the work.

---

## 30.4 Prevention Over Correction

The Company shall prefer preventing defects to correcting them.

The Company's quality system shall be designed so that problems are caught at their source — before they become defects, before they reach the customer. Where a problem does occur, the Company shall find its cause, correct it and learn from it, so that it does not recur.

---

## 30.5 Quality and the Company's Future

The Company's quality shall endure across generations.

The standards of the Company's quality are part of its constitution: they shall be preserved, upheld and improved by every generation. A Company that maintains its quality maintains its advantage; a Company that lets its quality slip loses what it was built upon.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **Quality is the Company's primary competitive advantage — the standard of the whole Company, managed deliberately, prevented at the source and proven by evidence.**
