# Chapter 50 — Constitutional Review

*This chapter explains how this Manual may evolve while preserving its founding principles. The Constitution is not frozen; it is a living document that changes deliberately — through formal amendment, never through silent change.*

---

## 50.1 The Constitution Evolves Deliberately

The Constitution shall evolve to meet the needs of each generation — but only through formal amendment, never through silent change.

The Company's principles are constant while its strategies, technologies and operations evolve. This Manual is the record of that constancy and that evolution: it shall grow with the Company, deliberately and transparently, so that its history is never obscured and its foundation is never weakened.

---

## 50.2 The Discipline of Amendment

Every amendment to this Constitution shall:

- **preserve the historical record** — no version shall be destroyed or concealed;
- **clearly identify the reason for change** — the purpose of the amendment shall be stated;
- **state the approving authority** — who approved the change shall be recorded;
- **identify the sections affected** — exactly what changed shall be clear;
- **strengthen, rather than weaken, the founding principles** — no amendment shall diminish the Company's foundation.

Amendments shall be recorded in the Revision History, with their reasons preserved so that future generations understand not only what changed, but why.

---

## 50.3 The Founding Principles Are Non-Negotiable

The Founding Principles shall not be weakened by amendment.

The Constitution may change in every other respect — its strategies, its structures, its expression — but the thirteen Founding Principles are the constant foundation upon which the Constitution rests. Any proposed change that would weaken a Founding Principle is not an amendment; it is a departure from the Company's foundation, and it shall not be made.

---

## 50.4 The Discipline of Review

The Constitution shall be reviewed deliberately, and amended when review shows amendment is needed.

The Company shall examine the Constitution against its experience — whether it serves the Company's purpose, whether its principles remain clear, and whether its provisions still meet the needs of the present generation. Review is an expression of stewardship: the Constitution is kept strong, not merely preserved.

---

## 50.5 The Constitution and the Future

This Constitution is written for those who will follow.

The Manual shall remain a living guide for future generations — a document they may develop, amend and improve, upon the foundation these principles provide. As long as the Company's principles endure, the Constitution may evolve with confidence: the Company changes its Manual deliberately, and its Manual protects the Company's constancy.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **The Constitution evolves by formal amendment, never by silent change — preserving its history, strengthening its principles, and remaining a living guide for every generation that follows.**
