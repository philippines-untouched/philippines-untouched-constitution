# Chapter 31 — Food Safety

*This chapter establishes food safety as a non-negotiable responsibility of the Company. The Company's products are food: they enter the bodies and the trust of the people who consume them. Nothing the Company does shall put that trust at risk.*

---

## 31.1 Food Safety Is Non-Negotiable

Food safety is not one priority among many. It is a non-negotiable responsibility of the Company.

The Company's products are consumed by real people — families, children, customers who trust the Company's name. That trust is the foundation of the Company's existence, and it rests, first and always, on the safety of what the Company produces.

No commercial objective, no deadline and no pressure shall ever compromise the safety of the Company's products.

---

## 31.2 The Responsibility Throughout the Chain

Food safety is the responsibility of every part of the Company, from field to customer.

The Company shall ensure the safety of its products at every stage:

- **in cultivation** — protecting produce from contamination from the start;
- **in manufacturing** — producing under clean, controlled and verifiable conditions;
- **in packaging and storage** — protecting products through handling and holding;
- **in transport** — maintaining the conditions that preserve safety;
- **at delivery** — ensuring products reach customers in a safe condition.

---

## 31.3 The Discipline of Food Safety

The Company's food safety shall be managed with discipline:

- **Prevention** — designing processes that prevent contamination before it can occur;
- **Control** — maintaining the conditions that keep products safe;
- **Verification** — testing, checking and recording that safety was maintained;
- **Traceability** — the ability to follow any product and act swiftly if needed;
- **Learning** — investigating any failure and correcting its cause, so that it does not recur;
- **Openness** — acting honestly and promptly when a safety concern arises, with those affected and with the authorities.

---

## 31.4 No Compromise

When a question of safety arises, the Company shall err on the side of safety.

If a product may be unsafe, the Company shall not release it — whatever the cost of withholding it. The Company shall recall what must be recalled, inform those who must be informed and correct the cause. The cost of protecting people is a cost the Company shall always bear.

---

## 31.5 Food Safety and the Company's Standards

Food safety is integral to the Company's quality and its integrity.

A product that is not safe is not quality, whatever its other merits. A Company that compromises safety has compromised everything it stands for. The Company's commitment to food safety is therefore absolute — it is the first duty of its quality and the deepest expression of its care for people.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **Food safety is non-negotiable. The Company shall never compromise the safety of its products — preventing harm, verifying its standards and always erring on the side of safety.**
