# Chapter 47 — Sustainable Profitability

*This chapter defines profit as the consequence of creating enduring value. The Company's profit is not its purpose — but it is the sign that the Company is fulfilling its purpose well enough to continue.*

---

## 47.1 Profit Is a Consequence

Profit is the consequence of creating enduring value — not the purpose of creating it.

When the Company produces quality, serves its customers honestly, stewards its resources and builds for the future, profit follows as the natural result of value well created. The Company's profitability is therefore both a measure of its work and a means of its continuation.

---

## 47.2 What Sustainable Profit Means

Sustainable profit is profit that can be earned again — and again — without depleting what it depends upon.

The Company's profitability shall be:

- **Earned** — the result of genuine value, not of shortcuts or deception;
- **Repeatable** — achievable consistently, not once at the cost of the future;
- **Sound** — resting on honest records and real performance;
- **Sufficient** — enough to sustain the Company, reward its people and invest in its future;
- **Stewardship-minded** — never earned by depleting the land, the people or the reputation.

---

## 47.3 The Role of Profit

Profit plays a necessary role in the Company's purpose:

- it sustains the Company's operations;
- it rewards those who contribute to its success;
- it funds investment in quality, people, land and capability;
- it builds the reserves that enable endurance;
- it provides the foundation for future generations.

A Company without profit cannot fulfil its purpose for long. The Company therefore seeks profit — as the means by which its purpose continues.

---

## 47.4 Profit and Principles

The Company shall not pursue profit in a manner inconsistent with its principles.

The Company shall decline profit that must be earned by sacrificing quality, safety, integrity, people or the land. Such profit is not sustainable — it is the consumption of the Company's future, disguised as earnings.

---

## 47.5 The Measure of the Company

The Company's success is measured by more than its profit.

Profit is one measure — the sign that the Company's value creation is sustainable. But the full measure of the Company's success is found in its enduring value: the quality of its products, the wellbeing of its people, the health of its land, the strength of its communities and the durability of its principles. Profit earned in the service of these is the profit the Company seeks.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **Profit is the consequence of creating enduring value. The Company earns profit sustainably — by serving its purpose — and never at the cost of its principles or its future.**
