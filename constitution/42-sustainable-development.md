# Chapter 42 — Sustainable Development

*This chapter defines the Company's commitment to balancing environmental, social and economic outcomes. The Company's development is sustainable when it can endure — meeting the needs of the present without depleting what the future depends upon.*

---

## 42.1 The Commitment to Sustainability

The Company is committed to sustainable development.

Sustainability is the Company's recognition that its long-term endurance depends upon the health of three foundations: the environment, the people and the economy. The Company's development shall balance these — never building one at the lasting expense of another.

---

## 42.2 The Three Dimensions

The Company's development shall balance:

- **Environmental outcomes** — protecting the land, water, biodiversity and resources upon which the Company depends;
- **Social outcomes** — serving its people, its communities and the wider society fairly and well;
- **Economic outcomes** — maintaining the financial strength that enables the Company to fulfil its purpose.

A Company that is environmentally sound but financially unsound cannot endure. A Company that is financially sound but environmentally destructive does not deserve to. Sustainability is the balance of all three, maintained together.

---

## 42.3 Development That Endures

The Company's development shall be measured by its endurance.

The Company shall ask of every development: can this be sustained for generations? A development that prospers briefly while depleting its foundations is not sustainable development — it is the consumption of the future.

---

## 42.4 Sustainability in Decision Making

Sustainability shall inform the Company's decisions.

The Company shall weigh the environmental, social and economic consequences of its significant decisions, in accordance with its Decision Framework, and shall choose the course that best sustains all three over the long term.

---

## 42.5 Sustainability and Stewardship

Sustainable development is the expression of the Company's stewardship.

What the Company develops, it holds in trust for those who follow. Sustainable development ensures that the Company's growth strengthens — rather than consumes — the environment, the people and the economy it depends upon, so that the Company's success can be inherited by future generations.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **The Company develops sustainably — balancing environmental, social and economic outcomes so that its success endures for generations rather than depleting the future.**
