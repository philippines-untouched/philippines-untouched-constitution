# Chapter 12 — Environmental Responsibility

*This chapter defines how the Company protects biodiversity, natural resources and ecological health. The Company's work depends upon a healthy environment, and the Company accepts responsibility for the environment it shares with all life.*

---

## 12.1 The Environment Is the Company's Foundation

The Company's work begins in nature. Its products are grown from the soil, nourished by water and sustained by the living systems around it.

A healthy environment is not a luxury for the Company; it is the foundation of the Company's existence. Environmental responsibility is therefore not an obligation imposed upon the Company's work — it is part of the Company's work.

---

## 12.2 Protection of Biodiversity

The Company shall protect the variety of life on which healthy ecosystems depend.

The Company shall:

- preserve and enhance the natural habitats within and around its land;
- protect native plant and animal life;
- avoid practices that harm pollinators, beneficial organisms or natural ecosystems;
- maintain and improve the ecological health of the land it manages;
- where practical, contribute to the restoration of ecosystems that have been degraded.

The Company recognises that biodiversity is not an obstacle to good agriculture; it is the partner of good agriculture.

---

## 12.3 Protection of Natural Resources

The Company shall use natural resources responsibly, so that they remain available to future generations.

The Company shall:

- **Protect soil health** — maintaining and improving the fertility of the soil;
- **Protect water** — using water carefully, protecting waterways from pollution, and safeguarding the water that communities and ecosystems depend upon;
- **Conserve energy** — using energy efficiently and preferring renewable sources where practical;
- **Reduce waste** — minimising waste in every activity and managing unavoidable waste responsibly;
- **Use materials responsibly** — preferring materials that can be renewed, reused or recovered.

---

## 12.4 Ecological Health

The Company shall manage its activities so that they sustain, rather than deplete, the health of the ecosystems it touches.

Ecological health is reflected in the condition of the soil, the quality of the water, the abundance of life and the resilience of the land. The Company shall monitor these conditions, understand the effects of its activities, and act on what it learns.

---

## 12.5 Environmental Responsibility in Practice

Environmental responsibility is a principle of daily work, not a separate programme.

Every activity of the Company — from cultivation to packaging to distribution — shall be conducted with regard for its environmental consequences. Where a choice exists between a practice that harms the environment and one that sustains it, the Company shall choose the practice that sustains.

The Company's environmental standards shall be consistent with its commitment to international best practice, and its performance shall be open to verification.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **Nature is the Company's foundation. The Company shall protect biodiversity, use natural resources responsibly and sustain the ecological health of the land and water entrusted to it.**
