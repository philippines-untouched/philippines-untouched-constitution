# Chapter 24 — International Strategy

*This chapter defines the Company's export aspirations and its commitment to meeting international standards. The Company seeks to carry authentic Philippine quality to the world, and to earn the trust of the world's most demanding markets.*

---

## 24.1 The International Aspiration

The Company's primary market is international export markets.

The Company aspires to bring the quality and authenticity of Philippine agriculture to customers around the world — competing in the premium segment through quality, trust and provenance, and earning international credibility through the standards it meets and the integrity of its conduct.

---

## 24.2 International Standards

The Company shall design its farming and quality systems around internationally recognised standards from the beginning.

The Company's commitment to international standards shall be demonstrated through:

- **Food safety** — meeting the safety requirements of its markets without compromise;
- **Traceability** — maintaining complete records from origin to customer, as export markets require;
- **Good Agricultural Practices** — farming to internationally recognised standards of practice;
- **Environmental stewardship** — responsible use of land, water and resources;
- **Worker welfare** — fair and safe conditions for the people who produce the Company's products;
- **Documented processes and verification** — systems that can be examined and proved;
- **Continuous improvement** — maintaining and raising its standards over time.

---

## 24.3 Certification as a Strategic Objective

Internationally recognised certification is a strategic objective of the Company, pursued for the substance it represents, not as a marketing badge.

The Company's pursuit of certification shall be genuine: the Company shall design its systems to meet the standard, and shall not claim certification until it has been formally obtained.

Until certification is formally granted, it shall be described as a strategic objective — never as an achieved status.

---

## 24.4 Export Discipline

Serving international markets is a discipline.

The Company shall:

- understand and meet the legal, regulatory and quality requirements of every market it serves;
- maintain the traceability and documentation that export markets require;
- represent its products honestly, in every market and every language;
- protect the reputation of Philippine quality by never shipping work that is below the Company's standard.

---

## 24.5 The World and the Company

The Company's international strategy serves its purpose.

Exporting strengthens the Company's endurance, shares the quality of Philippine agriculture with the world, and brings the discipline of international standards into the Company's daily work.

The Company enters international markets as itself — true to its principles, its provenance and its standards — and seeks to earn the same trust abroad that it builds at home.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **The Company seeks to carry authentic Philippine quality to the world, meeting internationally recognised standards — and shall claim no certification it has not formally earned.**
