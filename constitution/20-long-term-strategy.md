# Chapter 20 — Long-Term Strategy

*This chapter defines the Company's long-term direction, measured in decades rather than years. The Company's strategy serves its Purpose and moves it toward its Vision, without ever departing from its principles.*

---

## 20.1 Strategy Serves Purpose

Strategy is the Company's chosen path toward its Vision.

The Company's strategy shall serve its Purpose, move the Company toward its Vision, and remain consistent with its Founding Principles. A strategy that departs from the Company's principles is not a strategy; it is a mistake.

---

## 20.2 The Long-Term Horizon

The Company's direction is measured in decades, not in short-term cycles.

The Company shall make its strategic decisions with the long term in view:

- investments shall be judged by their contribution to the Company over generations;
- growth shall be pursued at a pace the Company can sustain;
- short-term results shall never be purchased at the expense of the Company's long-term health;
- the Company shall build capabilities and relationships that compound over time.

The Company accepts that long-term thinking may require patience — investments that mature slowly, standards that take time to earn, and restraint where short-term opportunity conflicts with enduring value.

---

## 20.3 Constancy and Evolution

The Company's principles are constant; its strategies evolve.

> **The principles of the Company shall remain constant while its strategies, technologies and operations evolve to meet the needs of each generation.**

The Company's strategy shall be reviewed and renewed as circumstances change — as markets evolve, as knowledge advances, and as each generation brings new understanding. What must not change is the foundation those strategies serve.

---

## 20.4 Strategy and Stewardship

Long-term strategy is an expression of stewardship.

The Company's strategic choices shall protect what is entrusted to it — the land, the people, the reputation and the Company itself — and shall leave each stronger for those who follow. No strategy shall enrich the present generation by consuming what future generations depend upon.

---

## 20.5 Strategic Discipline

The Company's long-term strategy shall be pursued with discipline:

- **Consistency** — the Company shall not abandon its direction with every change of circumstance;
- **Patience** — the Company shall allow its investments and its reputation to mature;
- **Learning** — the Company shall review its strategy against outcomes and adapt with understanding;
- **Integrity** — the Company shall pursue its strategy honestly, never at the cost of its principles.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **The Company's direction is measured in decades. Its principles remain constant while its strategies evolve to meet the needs of each generation.**
