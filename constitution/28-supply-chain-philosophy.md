# Chapter 28 — Supply Chain Philosophy

*This chapter explains how suppliers and logistics partners become extensions of the Company's values. The Company's responsibility does not end at its own gates; it extends through the chain of supply that brings products to customers.*

---

## 28.1 The Supply Chain as Part of the Company

The Company's supply chain — its suppliers, carriers, warehouses and logistics partners — is part of how the Company fulfils its purpose.

What the Company cannot produce itself, it sources; what it produces, it moves. The standards of the Company shall accompany its products and its practices through every link of that chain, from the sourcing of materials to the delivery of finished goods.

---

## 28.2 Suppliers as Extensions of the Company's Values

The Company shall choose and work with suppliers who share its commitment to quality, integrity and responsible practice.

The Company shall seek suppliers who:

- meet the Company's standards of quality and food safety;
- practise responsible stewardship of land, resources and people;
- conduct their business with honesty and transparency;
- can provide the evidence and traceability the Company requires;
- are willing to grow with the Company in the standards they meet.

The Company recognises that its standards extend to what it buys and from whom. A product sourced without integrity cannot be sold with integrity.

---

## 28.3 Partnerships of Respect

The Company's relationships with suppliers and logistics partners shall be honest, fair and enduring.

The Company shall:

- deal with its partners fairly, honouring its commitments and paying what is owed;
- communicate its standards clearly and support partners in meeting them;
- build relationships that benefit both parties over the long term;
- treat partners as contributors to the Company's success, not merely as vendors;
- prefer local suppliers where they can meet the Company's standards, in support of community prosperity.

---

## 28.4 Protecting Quality in Transit

The Company's logistics shall protect the quality and integrity of its products from the moment they leave the Company's hands until they reach the customer.

The Company shall ensure that:

- products are handled, stored and transported under conditions that preserve their quality and safety;
- products remain traceable through every stage of the journey;
- records accompany products so that their provenance and handling can be verified;
- the Company's standards are communicated to every partner who handles its products.

---

## 28.5 The Chain and the Company's Reputation

The Company's reputation is carried by its entire supply chain.

A failure anywhere in the chain — in quality, safety, honesty or practice — reflects on the Company that stands behind the product. The Company therefore holds its supply chain to its standards, works with its partners to maintain them, and accepts responsibility for the chain it has chosen.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **The Company's supply chain is an extension of the Company itself. Its suppliers and partners shall uphold the Company's standards of quality, integrity and responsible practice — from sourcing to delivery.**
