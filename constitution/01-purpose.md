# Chapter 1 — Purpose

*This chapter establishes why the Company exists. It defines the Company's reason for being beyond financial success, and sets the standard against which every decision, strategy and action of the Company shall be judged.*

---

## 1.1 The Reason for Existing

The Company was not conceived simply to make money.

The Company exists to create enduring value — value that extends beyond profit to the people who contribute to its success, the communities in which it operates, the land from which it draws its livelihood, and the generations that will follow.

The Company's purpose is to build a sustainable enterprise whose people, land, communities and principles are left stronger than they were found.

---

## 1.2 Profit Is a Means, Not the Purpose

Profit is necessary. A financially sustainable Company can provide employment, invest in its people, improve the land, develop products, innovate, support its communities and endure for generations. Without financial sustainability, the Company cannot fulfil its purpose.

Profit is a means to fulfilling the Company's purpose. It is not the purpose itself.

The Company shall not pursue profit in a manner that compromises its quality, its integrity, its people, its communities or the land entrusted to it.

---

## 1.3 The Value the Company Creates

The Company's purpose is fulfilled through the value it creates for:

- **Customers** — products and services of uncompromising quality, honestly presented;
- **Its people** — fair treatment, respect, dignity and appropriate reward for their contribution;
- **Suppliers and partners** — honest, enduring and mutually beneficial relationships;
- **Local communities** — meaningful employment, education and shared prosperity;
- **The natural environment** — responsible stewardship of the land, water and resources;
- **The Company itself** — sound governance, financial strength and institutional integrity;
- **Future generations** — a legacy of knowledge, reputation and opportunity that endures.

---

## 1.4 The Measure of Success

The Company's success shall be measured not only by its financial results, but by the health of the land, the wellbeing of its people, the strength of its communities, the trust of its customers and the durability of its principles.

A Company that is profitable but has weakened its land, its people or its reputation has not succeeded in fulfilling its purpose.

---

## 1.5 Constancy of Purpose

The Company's purpose shall remain constant.

Strategies, products, technologies and operations may evolve to meet the needs of each generation, but the reason the Company exists shall not change with markets, leadership, ownership or circumstance.

Every decision, at every level of the Company, shall be tested against the question: *does this strengthen the Company's ability to fulfil its purpose?*

---

## Foundational Principle

> **The Company exists to create enduring value.**
