# Chapter 39 — Digital Transformation

*This chapter explains how digital systems support decision making without replacing human judgement. The Company uses digital systems to inform, to record and to serve — while the judgement that matters remains human.*

---

## 39.1 Digital Systems Serve the Company

The Company's digital systems are tools of its work.

Digital systems shall support the Company's decisions, record its activities and serve its customers — in accordance with the Technology Philosophy. The purpose of digital transformation is not to become digital; it is to serve the Company's purpose better through the capabilities that digital systems provide.

---

## 39.2 Supporting Decision Making

Digital systems shall support decision making by providing trustworthy information.

The Company's digital systems shall:

- collect and preserve accurate evidence of the Company's activities;
- present information clearly, so that decisions are informed;
- make the Company's records accessible to those who need them;
- support traceability from origin to customer;
- help the Company observe its own operations and learn from them.

---

## 39.3 Judgement Remains Human

Digital systems inform decisions; they do not make them.

The Company's important decisions shall be made by people, exercising judgement in accordance with the Company's principles and its Decision Framework. No digital system shall replace the human judgement, the ethical responsibility or the accountability of the Company's people.

Digital systems may propose, analyse and inform. The decision — and the responsibility for it — remains with the people of the Company.

---

## 39.4 The Discipline of Digital Transformation

The Company's digital transformation shall be disciplined:

- **Purposeful** — adopted for the value it creates, in accordance with the Technology Philosophy;
- **Gradual** — introduced in steps that can be understood, verified and corrected;
- **Evidence-preserving** — never at the cost of the accuracy or permanence of the Company's records;
- **Human-centred** — designed to serve the people who use it and the customers it serves;
- **Enduring** — chosen and built so that the Company can maintain it across generations.

---

## 39.5 Digital Systems and the Company's Memory

Digital systems are instruments of the Company's organisational memory.

The Company's digital systems shall preserve its knowledge, its records and its history — so that the Company's memory does not depend upon any individual, and the knowledge of one generation becomes an asset available to the next.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **Digital systems support the Company's decisions with trustworthy information — while the judgement, the ethics and the accountability remain human.**
