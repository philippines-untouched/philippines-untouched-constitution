# Chapter 10 — People Grow Prosperity

*This chapter defines the Company's belief that investing in people creates enduring business success. The people who contribute to the Company's success are not a cost to be minimised; they are the Company's most important investment.*

---

## 10.1 The Belief

The Company believes that people grow prosperity.

Enduring business success is not built by machines, processes or capital alone. It is built by people — their skill, their care, their judgement and their commitment. A Company that invests in its people invests in its own future.

The Company's people include its employees, contractors, consultants, partners and all who contribute meaningfully to its work.

---

## 10.2 What the Company Provides

The Company shall seek to provide its people with:

- **Fair and dignified remuneration** — reward that reflects the value of their contribution;
- **Safe working conditions** — a workplace that protects their health and wellbeing;
- **Respect** — treatment with the dignity every person deserves;
- **Professional development** — opportunities to grow in skill and capability;
- **Education and training** — the knowledge needed to do their work well and to progress;
- **Recognition** — acknowledgement of good work and contribution;
- **Career opportunities** — a path for those who wish to advance;
- **Appropriate participation in the Company's success** — a fair share of the value they help create.

---

## 10.3 Reciprocal Responsibility

The relationship between the Company and its people is reciprocal:

> **The Company shall not expect a level of commitment from its people that it is unwilling to honour with fairness, respect and integrity.**

This principle does not guarantee lifetime employment or identical outcomes for every person. It establishes a mutual obligation: the Company honours its people's commitment with fairness, and its people contribute their commitment to the Company's work.

---

## 10.4 Difficult Decisions

When difficult employment decisions are unavoidable, they shall be approached with honesty, fairness, respect and dignity, and with consideration for those affected.

A Company that treats its people well in good times demonstrates its character. A Company that treats its people with dignity in difficult times demonstrates its integrity.

---

## 10.5 Investment, Not Cost

The Company shall regard investment in its people as investment in the Company itself.

Training, education and development strengthen the capability of the Company and its communities. A person who grows in skill and understanding contributes more to the Company and carries that capability forward through their work and the people they teach.

The Company's prosperity is grown by its people; the Company's duty is to grow its people in return.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **People grow prosperity. The Company shall invest in its people — with fairness, respect, dignity and appropriate reward — and shall not expect a level of commitment it is unwilling to honour.**
