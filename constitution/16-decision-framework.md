# Chapter 16 — Decision Framework

*This chapter provides the structured process by which significant business decisions are evaluated. The Company's decisions shall flow from its principles, be supported by evidence, and remain defensible to future generations.*

---

## 16.1 Decisions Flow from Principles

The Company's decisions are expressions of its principles.

Every significant decision of the Company shall be made in accordance with the Founding Principles and the Core Values. A decision that conflicts with the Company's principles is not a valid option; it is a departure from the Company's foundation.

---

## 16.2 The Framework

A significant decision of the Company shall be considered against the following questions:

1. **Does it improve quality?**
2. **Does it strengthen trust?**
3. **Does it protect nature?**
4. **Does it respect people?**
5. **Does it strengthen the community?**
6. **Is it supported by evidence?**
7. **Is it financially sustainable?**
8. **Does it strengthen the Company for the future?**
9. **Would the Company be comfortable explaining the decision publicly?**
10. **Would the decision still appear correct to a future generation?**

A decision that fails a question shall be reconsidered. A decision that fails a question involving a Founding Principle shall not proceed.

---

## 16.3 Evidence and Understanding

The Company shall understand the whole system before solving individual problems.

Decisions shall be based on trustworthy evidence, not assumption. The Company shall seek to understand the context, the consequences and the alternatives before deciding, and shall record the decision with its reasons so that future generations can understand not only what was decided, but why.

---

## 16.4 The Discipline of Decision Making

The Company's decision making shall be disciplined:

- **Timely** — decisions made when they are needed, not deferred until forced;
- **Proportionate** — effort of deliberation matched to the significance of the decision;
- **Recorded** — significant decisions documented with their reasoning;
- **Owned** — every decision has a clear owner who is accountable for it;
- **Reviewed** — significant decisions reviewed in the light of their outcomes, so that the Company learns.

---

## 16.5 Decisions and the Future

A decision of the Company should be one the Company would be willing to defend to a future generation.

This test guards against decisions that serve short-term convenience at the expense of the Company's enduring interests. When the Company's leaders decide, they decide on behalf of the generations that will follow, and their decisions shall reflect that responsibility.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **The Company's decisions flow from its principles, are supported by evidence, and are made in a manner the Company would be proud to explain to a future generation.**
