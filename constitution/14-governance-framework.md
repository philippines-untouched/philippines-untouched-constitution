# Chapter 14 — Governance Framework

*This chapter defines how authority, accountability and responsibility are exercised throughout the Company. Governance is how the Company ensures that power is exercised well — in accordance with its principles, for the benefit of the Company itself, and never above the law or above the Company's own standards.*

---

## 14.1 The Purpose of Governance

Governance is the framework through which the Company directs and controls its affairs.

The purpose of governance is to ensure that the Company:

- is directed in accordance with its Purpose, Vision and Founding Principles;
- exercises authority with accountability;
- is transparent in its significant decisions;
- manages its affairs responsibly;
- and endures beyond the service of any individual.

Although the Company is a One Person Corporation, it shall operate with governance standards appropriate to the enduring institution it seeks to become.

---

## 14.2 The Principles of Governance

The Company's governance rests on these principles:

- **Accountability** — every exercise of authority is answerable for its exercise;
- **Transparency** — significant decisions and their reasons are documented and open to appropriate scrutiny;
- **Ethical leadership** — those who lead are bound by the Company's values and principles;
- **Documented decision making** — significant decisions are recorded with their reasoning;
- **Responsible financial management** — the Company's resources are managed with care and integrity;
- **Clear delegation** — authority is delegated explicitly, with corresponding accountability;
- **Risk management** — risks are understood, managed and balanced against opportunity;
- **Succession** — the orderly transfer of leadership is planned and prepared;
- **Long-term stewardship** — governance serves the Company's long-term endurance, not short-term convenience.

---

## 14.3 Authority, Accountability and Responsibility

Authority, accountability and responsibility are distinct and shall be clearly defined:

- **Authority** — the power to make decisions and commit the Company's resources, granted explicitly;
- **Accountability** — the obligation to answer for the exercise of authority;
- **Responsibility** — the duty to perform a task or achieve an outcome.

No authority shall be exercised without corresponding accountability. No person shall hold authority without being answerable for its use.

Delegation of authority does not diminish accountability. The person who delegates remains accountable for the exercise of the authority they have granted.

---

## 14.4 No One Is Above the Company's Principles

No individual is above the Company's governing principles.

This applies to owners, directors, executives, officers, managers and founders alike. The Company's principles govern the conduct of everyone who acts on its behalf, and the Company's governance shall not be bent to serve any individual's interest above the Company's own.

---

## 14.5 Governance and the Future

The Company's governance is designed for continuity.

The framework shall ensure that the Company's direction, its records, its knowledge and its leadership can pass from one generation to the next without interruption. Governance is the mechanism by which the Company remains the same institution, true to the same principles, whoever holds its offices at any time.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **Governance is the framework by which the Company directs its affairs with accountability, transparency and long-term stewardship — and no individual is above the Company's principles.**
