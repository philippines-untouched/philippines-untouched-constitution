# Chapter 26 — Agricultural Philosophy

*This chapter explains how farming decisions should support quality, sustainability and long-term land stewardship. The Company's agriculture is the foundation of its products — and the beginning of its responsibility.*

---

## 26.1 The Foundation of the Company's Work

Agriculture is the foundation of the Company's work.

The Company's products begin in the soil and are shaped by the way they are grown. The Company's agricultural philosophy therefore determines the quality of its products, the health of its land and the integrity of its provenance.

---

## 26.2 The Principles of the Company's Agriculture

The Company's farming shall be:

- **Quality-driven** — grown for quality of produce, not quantity alone;
- **Sustainable** — capable of continuing for generations without depleting the land;
- **Stewardship-minded** — leaving the land healthier than it was found;
- **Evidence-based** — practices recorded, verified and improved with knowledge;
- **Responsible** — protecting soil, water, biodiversity and the people who work the land;
- **Internationally grounded** — consistent with recognised Good Agricultural Practices.

---

## 26.3 Farming Decisions

Farming decisions shall support quality, sustainability and long-term land stewardship.

The Company shall choose its crops, its methods and its practices with care for:

- **Product quality** — growing what the Company can grow well;
- **Soil health** — protecting and improving the fertility of the land;
- **Water** — using water responsibly and protecting waterways;
- **Biodiversity** — sustaining the living systems that support the farm;
- **People** — safe and fair conditions for those who work the land;
- **The future** — leaving the land productive for generations to come.

---

## 26.4 Good Agricultural Practice

The Company shall farm in accordance with recognised Good Agricultural Practices.

Good Agricultural Practice is the disciplined application of knowledge to farming: understanding the land, managing inputs responsibly, protecting food safety, preserving the environment and caring for workers — with documented processes and verifiable evidence.

The Company shall design its farming systems around such internationally recognised standards from the beginning, and shall pursue certification as a strategic objective, never claiming it before it is formally obtained.

---

## 26.5 The Land and the Seasons

The Company's agriculture shall work with the land and the seasons, not against them.

The Company shall respect the natural rhythms of its environment, choose practices suited to its place, and farm in a manner that can be sustained over decades. Agriculture that fights the land may prosper briefly; agriculture that works with the land endures.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **The Company's agriculture is the foundation of its products and the beginning of its responsibility — farmed for quality, sustained for generations, and grounded in Good Agricultural Practice.**
