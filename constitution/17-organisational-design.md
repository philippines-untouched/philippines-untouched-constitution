# Chapter 17 — Organisational Design

*This chapter explains how the Company is designed as an integrated system rather than a collection of independent departments. The Company's strength lies in how its parts work together in service of the whole.*

---

## 17.1 The Company Is One System

The Company is one integrated system, not a collection of disconnected departments or activities.

Every part of the Company — farming, manufacturing, quality, sales, technology, finance and all others — serves the same Purpose, follows the same principles and depends upon the others. The Company's success is the success of the whole system, and no part of the Company succeeds at the expense of another.

---

## 17.2 Understand the Whole First

The Company's approach to its own design follows the principle:

> **Understand the whole system first, then address it one meaningful part at a time.**

Before changing any part of the Company, the Company shall understand how that part fits within the whole — what it contributes, what it depends upon, and how changes to it affect the rest of the system.

---

## 17.3 One Part at a Time

Large problems are addressed one meaningful part at a time.

The Company does not attempt to change everything at once. It identifies the parts of the system that most need attention, improves them deliberately, and verifies that each improvement strengthens the whole.

---

## 17.4 Complexity That Serves Value

The Company's design shall favour simplicity.

Complexity shall exist only where it creates value — where it improves quality, strengthens trust, protects nature or serves the Company's purpose. Unnecessary complexity shall continually be removed.

The Company shall prefer:

- **Clear structures** — where roles, authority and accountabilities are easily understood;
- **Simple processes** — where work flows without unnecessary steps;
- **Integrated systems** — where information and activity connect rather than fragment;
- **Maintainable design** — where the Company can understand, sustain and improve its own operation.

---

## 17.5 Design That Serves the Future

The Company's organisational design shall serve the Company's long-term endurance.

The Company shall be designed so that:

- no part of the Company depends upon a single individual;
- knowledge and capability are held by the system, not only by persons;
- the Company can grow, evolve and reorganise without losing its integrity;
- each generation can understand the Company's design and improve it deliberately.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **The Company is one integrated system. It shall understand the whole before addressing any part, keep complexity where it creates value, and remove it where it does not.**
