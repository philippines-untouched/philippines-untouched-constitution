# Chapter 38 — Technology Philosophy

*This chapter defines technology as an enabler of quality, efficiency and organisational learning. The Company uses technology in service of its purpose — never as an end in itself, and never for its own sake.*

---

## 38.1 Technology Is an Enabler

Technology is a means, not an end.

The Company uses technology to enable its purpose: to produce quality, to strengthen trust, to learn and to endure. Technology that serves the Company's purpose is adopted; technology that does not — however advanced — is not needed.

---

## 38.2 What Technology Shall Support

The Company's technology shall support:

- **Quality** — helping the Company meet and verify its standards;
- **Traceability** — enabling complete records from origin to customer;
- **Efficiency** — helping the Company do its work well with the least necessary effort;
- **Automation** — performing tasks reliably where automation serves the Company;
- **Decision making** — providing trustworthy information for decisions;
- **Organisational memory** — preserving the Company's knowledge and history;
- **Customer experience** — serving customers honestly and well;
- **Scalability** — enabling the Company to grow without losing its standards.

---

## 38.3 The Discipline of Technology

The Company's technology shall be adopted with discipline:

- **Purposeful** — adopted for the value it creates, never for novelty;
- **Simplifying** — technology shall simplify operations where possible;
- **Proportionate** — complexity shall be introduced only where it creates meaningful value;
- **Evidence-based** — adopted on tested understanding, not fashion or assumption;
- **Gradual** — introduced in steps that can be understood, verified and corrected.

---

## 38.4 The Systems the Company Favours

The Company shall favour technology that is:

- **Reliable** — performing consistently, so the Company can depend upon it;
- **Auditable** — leaving evidence that can be examined and verified;
- **Observable** — understandable in its operation and its results;
- **Maintainable** — capable of being sustained, repaired and improved;
- **Standards-based** — built on recognised standards, not proprietary lock-in;
- **Interoperable** — capable of working with other systems over the long term.

---

## 38.5 Technology and the Future

Technology shall serve the Company across generations.

The Company shall choose technology that can endure — that the Company can understand, maintain and hand on, without dependence upon any individual or any single supplier. Technology may change; the principles the Company applies to it shall not.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **Technology is an enabler of the Company's purpose — adopted for the value it creates, kept simple where possible, and chosen to serve the Company across generations.**
