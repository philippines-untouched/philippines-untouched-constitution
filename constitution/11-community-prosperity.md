# Chapter 11 — Community Prosperity

*This chapter explains the Company's commitment to strengthening the communities in which it operates. The Company's relationship with its communities is not charity; it is shared prosperity — built through employment, education and opportunity.*

---

## 11.1 Shared Prosperity, Not Charity

The Company's philosophy towards its communities is:

> **The goal is not charity. The goal is shared prosperity.**

Charity gives; shared prosperity grows. The Company seeks to create lasting opportunities that strengthen its communities' own capability, so that the benefit of the Company's presence endures beyond any single act of giving.

The Company succeeds when its communities succeed, and it seeks to build that success together.

---

## 11.2 Creating Lasting Opportunity

The Company shall seek to create lasting opportunities through:

- **Meaningful employment** — work that provides genuine livelihood and dignity;
- **Fair remuneration** — reward that reflects the value of the work;
- **Local purchasing** — sourcing from local businesses where practical and consistent with the Company's standards;
- **Support for local businesses** — strengthening the enterprises around the Company;
- **Agricultural knowledge sharing** — passing on good practices and lessons learned;
- **Education** — supporting learning at every level;
- **Student placements, internships and apprenticeships** — practical paths into skilled work;
- **Agricultural research** — contributing to the knowledge of Philippine agriculture;
- **Practical learning opportunities** — real experience, not theory alone.

---

## 11.3 Learning Together

The Company may build relationships with agricultural schools, colleges, universities, training institutions and local agricultural organisations.

Educational initiatives may include student work placements, internships, farm visits, educational tours, practical agricultural demonstrations, exposure to Good Agricultural Practices, exposure to internationally recognised quality systems, and research collaboration.

The longer-term aspiration is for the Company to become a recognised **Centre of Excellence for sustainable Philippine agriculture** — a place where students, farmers, researchers and industry professionals can learn, observe and contribute.

---

## 11.4 Capability, Not Dependency

The objective of the Company's community commitment is to build capability, not dependency.

The Company seeks to strengthen the ability of its communities to prosper on their own terms. Knowledge shared, skills taught and opportunities created are of greater and longer value than assistance that leaves a community dependent upon its giver.

---

## 11.5 The Company and Its Communities

The Company's communities are part of its purpose. The value the Company creates extends beyond its customers to the people and places around it.

The Company shall conduct itself so that its presence strengthens its communities — through the work it provides, the knowledge it shares, the businesses it supports and the opportunity it creates — for as long as the Company operates.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **The goal is not charity; the goal is shared prosperity. The Company shall build capability in its communities through employment, education and opportunity.**
