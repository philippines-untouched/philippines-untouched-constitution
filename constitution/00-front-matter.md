# Chapter 0.2 — Document Control

## Purpose

This Manual is the highest governing document of Philippines Untouched OPC.

Its purpose is to establish the enduring principles, governance framework and strategic direction upon which the Company is founded.

Unlike operational documents, this Manual is intended to remain relevant for generations. While business plans, procedures, technologies and organisational structures may evolve, they shall do so in a manner that remains consistent with the principles contained herein.

This Manual is not intended to describe how individual tasks are performed. Rather, it explains why the Company exists, what it believes, how it governs itself and the standards by which its success shall be measured.

---

## Authority

The Corporate Governance & Strategy Manual is recognised as the constitutional document of Philippines Untouched OPC.

All subordinate documents derive their authority from this Manual, including but not limited to:

* Articles of Incorporation
* By-Laws
* Board or Shareholder Resolutions
* Corporate Policies
* Business Plans
* Strategic Plans
* Financial Plans
* Operational Manuals
* Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
* Quality Management Systems
* Food Safety Systems
* GLOBAL G.A.P. Documentation
* Human Resource Policies
* Brand Standards
* Technology Standards
* Product Business Plans

Where a conflict exists between this Manual and a subordinate document, the principles contained within this Manual shall prevail unless formally amended.

---

## Scope

This Manual applies to every person acting on behalf of Philippines Untouched OPC.

This includes:

* Directors
* Officers
* Executives
* Employees
* Contractors
* Consultants
* Volunteers
* Business partners where contractual obligations require adherence to Company standards

Leadership carries a particular responsibility to uphold both the letter and the spirit of this Manual.

---

## Nature of this Manual

This Manual is intended to be:

* enduring rather than temporary;
* principle-based rather than procedural;
* strategic rather than operational;
* clear rather than complex;
* adaptable without compromising its core philosophy.

It is expected that future generations will revise this Manual as circumstances evolve. Such revisions should strengthen the Company's ability to fulfil its purpose while preserving its founding principles.

---

## Stewardship

This Manual is entrusted to every generation of leadership.

Each generation inherits not only the responsibility to operate the Company successfully, but also the obligation to preserve and strengthen this Manual before passing it to those who follow.

No amendment should diminish the integrity, purpose or founding philosophy of Philippines Untouched.

# Chapter 0.3 — Revision History

## Purpose

A Company that values integrity must also value the integrity of its own history.

This Manual shall maintain a complete and permanent record of every formal amendment made throughout its lifetime.

No previous version shall be destroyed or intentionally concealed.

The evolution of the Company's governance forms part of its institutional knowledge and should remain available to future leaders seeking to understand not only what decisions were made, but why they were made.

Revision history preserves accountability, supports organisational learning and demonstrates respect for those who have served as custodians of the Company before us.

## Amendment Principles

Every amendment shall:

* preserve the historical record;
* clearly identify the reason for change;
* state the approving authority;
* identify affected sections;
* strengthen, rather than weaken, the constitutional principles of the Company.

The Manual shall never be rewritten in a manner that obscures its history.

## Foundational Principle

> *An organisation that preserves its history preserves its wisdom.*
