# Chapter 43 — Climate Resilience

*This chapter explains how the Company prepares for environmental change while protecting long-term productivity. The Company's future depends on its ability to endure change — understanding the climate it farms in, preparing for its variability, and strengthening the land and the Company against what may come.*

---

## 43.1 Preparing for Change

The climate is not static, and the Company's planning shall not assume it is.

The Company shall understand the environmental conditions in which it operates, prepare for their variability, and build the capacity to endure change — so that the Company's long-term productivity is protected whatever the seasons bring.

---

## 43.2 Understanding the Environment

The Company shall understand the environment it depends upon:

- the patterns of its climate and their variability;
- the risks that environmental change poses to its land, its water and its crops;
- the practices that strengthen or weaken its resilience;
- the knowledge available from experience, research and good practice.

Understanding precedes preparation. The Company cannot prepare for what it has not taken the trouble to understand.

---

## 43.3 Building Resilience

The Company shall build its resilience deliberately:

- **Strengthening the land** — healthy soil, protected biodiversity and sound water management make the land more able to endure stress;
- **Diversifying wisely** — variety in crops and practices that spreads risk without abandoning the Company's standards;
- **Preparing for variability** — planning for the range of conditions the Company may face, not only the expected;
- **Maintaining reserves** — the financial and operational strength to absorb difficulty;
- **Learning from experience** — recording what environmental events teach, so that each generation farms wiser than the last.

---

## 43.4 Protecting Long-Term Productivity

Climate resilience serves the Company's long-term productivity.

The Company shall prepare for environmental change in a manner that protects its ability to produce quality products for generations — never trading long-term resilience for short-term convenience, and never depleting the land's capacity to recover.

---

## 43.5 Resilience and Stewardship

Climate resilience is an expression of the Company's stewardship.

The Company's preparation for change is preparation on behalf of those who follow: the land is strengthened, the knowledge is preserved and the Company is made more able to endure — so that future generations inherit a Company better prepared for the climate they will face.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **The Company prepares for environmental change — understanding its environment, strengthening the land and the Company, and protecting long-term productivity for the generations that follow.**
