# Chapter 36 — Brand Architecture

*This chapter explains the relationship between Philippines Untouched, PUM and future brands. The Company's brands are expressions of the Company itself — related to one another deliberately, and all bound by the same principles.*

---

## 36.1 The Architecture of the Company's Brands

The Company presents itself through a deliberate architecture of names and brands:

- **Philippines Untouched OPC** — the legal identity of the Company, used for corporate, legal and regulatory purposes;
- **Philippines Untouched** — the corporate identity and trading name, under which the Company conducts its business;
- **PUM** — the principal consumer and retail brand, under which the Company's products are presented to customers;
- **Future product brands and sub-brands** — additional brands the Company may establish to serve specific products, ranges or markets.

Each element of the architecture serves a distinct purpose, and together they present the Company coherently to the world.

---

## 36.2 One Company, Many Expressions

The Company's brands are expressions of one Company.

Whatever name or brand a customer encounters, they encounter the same Company — bound by the same Purpose, the same principles and the same standards. No brand is separate from the Company's integrity, and no brand may present the Company in a way that conflicts with what the Company is.

---

## 36.3 The Role of PUM

PUM is the Company's principal consumer and retail brand.

PUM is the name by which most customers encounter the Company's products. It shall be built around:

- **Authentic Philippine provenance** — products genuinely and honestly of the Philippines;
- **Premium quality** — uncompromising standards in everything presented under the brand;
- **Trust** — conduct and records that customers can rely upon;
- **Transparency** — honest presentation of products, practices and provenance;
- **Craftsmanship** — care and skill in production, at every stage;
- **Responsible production** — stewardship of the land, the people and the resources involved;
- **Sustainability** — production that can endure without depleting what it depends upon.

---

## 36.4 Future Brands

The Company may establish future product brands and sub-brands as its product range grows.

Future brands shall be established deliberately, each serving a clear purpose within the architecture. Every future brand shall:

- remain consistent with the Company's principles and standards;
- serve the Company's Purpose and strengthen its reputation;
- be clearly related to the Company within the architecture;
- never weaken the trust attached to the Company's established names.

No brand is subordinate to another brand. Every brand — PUM included — relates to the Company directly, and derives its standing from the Company's principles and reputation.

---

## 36.5 Defined by Behaviour, Not Identity

The Company's brands are defined by behaviour and standards, not merely visual identity.

A brand is not its logo or its packaging; it is the accumulated experience of everyone who deals with it. The Company's brands shall therefore be built and maintained through the Company's conduct — its quality, its integrity and its evidence — in every product and every promise.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **The Company's brands are deliberate expressions of one Company — related within a clear architecture, built on behaviour and standards, and all bound by the same principles.**
