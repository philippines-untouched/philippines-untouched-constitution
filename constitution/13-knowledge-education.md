# Chapter 13 — Knowledge & Education

*This chapter explains the Company's commitment to continual learning, agricultural education and knowledge sharing. Knowledge is a living asset of the Company: it grows with use, is preserved for the future, and is shared to strengthen the people and communities around the Company.*

---

## 13.1 The Commitment to Learning

The Company is committed to continual learning.

Agriculture, food production and the standards of the Company's industry are never static. The Company shall learn continuously — from its own experience, from the evidence it records, from the expertise of its people and from the wider knowledge of its field — and shall apply what it learns to its daily work.

The Company's commitment to learning is not limited to formal education. It is a discipline of daily work: to observe, to understand, to improve and to share.

---

## 13.2 Knowledge Is a Strategic Asset

The knowledge the Company accumulates — its practices, its lessons, its records and its understanding — is a strategic asset, as valuable as its land, its equipment and its reputation.

Knowledge shall not be allowed to disappear when individuals leave. The Company shall preserve what it learns through:

- **Documentation** — recording practices, decisions and lessons in durable form;
- **Evidence** — maintaining the records that support the Company's knowledge;
- **Decision records** — preserving not only what was decided, but why;
- **Process knowledge** — capturing how the Company's work is performed well;
- **Historical records** — preserving the Company's history as institutional memory;
- **Training** — transferring knowledge to those who will carry it forward;
- **Knowledge transfer** — ensuring that knowledge passes deliberately from one generation to the next.

The knowledge accumulated by one generation shall become an asset available to the next. The Company shall be capable of learning over decades.

---

## 13.3 Agricultural Education

The Company is committed to agricultural education — within its own operations and in the communities around it.

The Company shall:

- educate and train its own people in good agricultural practice and the standards of the Company's industry;
- provide practical learning opportunities — placements, internships, apprenticeships and demonstrations — for students and those entering the field;
- share its knowledge of good practice with farmers and agricultural professionals;
- support the learning institutions and organisations that educate the next generation of Philippine agriculture.

Through this commitment, the Company contributes to the knowledge and capability of Philippine agriculture as a whole.

---

## 13.4 Knowledge Sharing

Knowledge that is shared grows; knowledge that is hoarded withers.

The Company shall share its knowledge where sharing strengthens its people, its communities or the practice of good agriculture — while protecting the proprietary information essential to the Company's competitiveness.

Sharing is consistent with the Company's philosophy of shared prosperity: the Company's knowledge is part of what it contributes to the communities it serves.

---

## 13.5 Learning and the Future Leader

The Company's commitment to knowledge and education is a commitment to the future.

A future leader of the Company shall inherit not only the Company's assets but its knowledge — the accumulated understanding of how the Company works, why it makes its decisions and what it has learned. That inheritance is one of the greatest the Company can pass on.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **Knowledge should be cultivated and shared. The Company shall learn continually, preserve what it learns, and pass it on to the generations that follow.**
