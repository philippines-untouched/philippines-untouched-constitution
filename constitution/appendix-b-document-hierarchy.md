## B.2 How Authority Flows

The Constitution is the source of authority for every document below it.

- **The Constitution provides principles** — what the Company is, what it believes, how it governs, where it is going, and the standards by which it operates.
- **Subordinate documents provide implementation** — how the Company's principles are carried out in each domain.

Every subordinate document shall be consistent with the Constitution. Where a conflict exists, the principles of the Constitution shall prevail unless formally amended (see Chapter 0.2 and Chapter 0.6).

---

## B.3 The Levels of the Hierarchy

**The Constitution (Level 1)**
The Corporate Governance & Strategy Manual — the highest governing document. Establishes enduring principles across eleven Parts and the Front Matter.

**Corporate Instruments (Level 2)**
Documents that define the Company's legal and governing framework:

- Articles of Incorporation
- By-Laws
- Corporate Policies
- Board or Shareholder Resolutions

**Strategy and Plans (Level 3)**
Documents that translate the Company's strategic direction into plans:

- Five-Year Strategic Plan
- Annual Business Plan
- Budget
- Product Business Plans

**Operational and Quality Systems (Level 4)**
Documents that govern the Company's work:

- Farm Manual, Manufacturing Manual, Export Manual, Logistics Manual
- Quality Management System, Food Safety System, GLOBAL G.A.P. Documentation
- Traceability and Internal Audit systems
- Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

**Brand and Technology Standards (Level 4)**
Documents that govern the Company's presentation and enablement:

- PUM Brand Standards, Packaging Standards, Marketing Guidelines
- Technology Architecture, Data Standards, Security, Automation

---

## B.4 The Discipline of the Hierarchy

The hierarchy is maintained with discipline:

- **No contradiction** — no subordinate document may contradict the Constitution;
- **No silent departure** — where a subordinate document must differ, the matter is resolved through the Constitution's amendment discipline, not by silent exception;
- **Clear derivation** — every subordinate document should be traceable to the principles it implements;
- **Orderly evolution** — the Constitution changes by formal amendment; subordinate documents change under their own governance, always consistent with the Constitution.

---

## B.5 The Hierarchy and the Future

The hierarchy shall endure across generations.

Future leaders shall find in the Constitution the principles that govern every document of the Company, and in the subordinate documents the implementation suited to their time. The hierarchy is part of the Company's design as an integrated system — every level serving the whole, and the whole defined by its Constitution.
