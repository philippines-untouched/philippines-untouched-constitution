# Appendix D — Referenced Standards

*This appendix lists the legislation, international standards and recognised frameworks that influence the Company's governance and strategic direction. Listing a standard here is a statement of influence and intent — not a claim that the Company is certified to it. Certification is a strategic objective until formally obtained (see Chapters 24 and 32).*

---

## D.1 Philippine Legislation

The Company is organised and operates under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, including:

| Reference | Relevance |
|---|---|
| **Republic Act No. 11232** — Revised Corporation Code of the Philippines | Establishes the legal framework for corporations, including the One Person Corporation (OPC) form under which Philippines Untouched OPC is organised. |
| **Republic Act No. 10611** — Food Safety Act of 2013 | Establishes the framework for food safety regulation and the responsibilities of food businesses in the Philippines. |
| **Republic Act No. 7394** — Consumer Act of the Philippines | Establishes consumer protection standards and the obligations of businesses toward consumers. |
| **Republic Act No. 8550, as amended** — Philippine Fisheries Code | Applicable where the Company's activities touch Philippine aquatic and fisheries resources. |
| Applicable environmental legislation | Laws governing environmental protection, resource management and land stewardship applicable to the Company's operations. |

*The Company shall comply with all applicable Philippine laws, and this list shall be maintained as legislation evolves.*

## D.2 International Certification Standards — Strategic Objectives

The Company designs its systems around internationally recognised standards. Certification under these standards is a strategic objective, pursued for the substance it represents — and shall never be implied as achieved until formally obtained by the certifying body (see Chapters 24 and 32):

| Standard | Relevance |
|---|---|
| **GLOBAL G.A.P. Integrated Farm Assurance (IFA) Smart Version 6** | The internationally recognised standard for Good Agricultural Practices; the Company's primary certification objective for international export markets. |
| Other internationally recognised certification schemes | Additional certifications may be pursued where they serve the Company's purpose and markets, each as a strategic objective until formally obtained. |

---

## D.4 How This Appendix Is Used

This appendix is a reference, not an assertion.

- **Influence and intent** — listing a standard records that it influences the Company's direction.
- **No implied certification** — nothing in this appendix implies that any certification, accreditation or recognition has been achieved.
- **Formal status recorded separately** — when a certification is formally obtained, its status shall be recorded through the amendment process (Revision History, Chapter 0.3), never by silent assumption.
- **Maintained deliberately** — this appendix shall be updated as legislation, standards and the Company's strategic objectives evolve.
