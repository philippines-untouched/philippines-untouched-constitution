# Chapter 46 — Capital Allocation

*This chapter explains how financial resources should be invested responsibly for long-term benefit. The Company's capital is a tool of its purpose, and how the Company deploys it determines what the Company becomes.*

---

## 46.1 Capital Serves the Company's Future

The Company's capital — its financial resources — shall be allocated in service of the Company's purpose and its long-term endurance.

Capital is not held for its own sake, nor spent for its own sake. It is invested where it will create the most enduring value: strengthening quality, building capability, serving people, stewarding the land and securing the Company's future.

---

## 46.2 The Principles of Allocation

The Company's allocation of capital shall be:

- **Purposeful** — invested in what serves the Company's Purpose and principles;
- **Long-term** — weighed against the benefit over decades, not quarters;
- **Stewardship-minded** — invested so that what is entrusted is left stronger;
- **Balanced** — distributed among the Company's needs with judgement, not concentrated recklessly;
- **Evidence-based** — decided on trustworthy information and recorded with reasoning;
- **Honest** — invested in what is real, never in the appearance of value.

---

## 46.3 What the Company Invests In

The Company shall invest its capital in:

- **Quality and safety** — the systems and standards that protect the Company's products and reputation;
- **People** — the development and fair reward of those who build the Company;
- **Land and resources** — the stewardship that sustains the Company's foundation;
- **Capability** — the knowledge, technology and capacity that enable the Company to improve;
- **Resilience** — the strength to endure difficulty without compromising principles;
- **The Company itself** — the governance, memory and institutions that secure its future.

---

## 46.4 The Discipline of Investment

The Company's investments shall be made with discipline:

- **Understood** — the Company understands what it invests in and why;
- **Proportionate** — investment matched to the value and risk it carries;
- **Reviewed** — investments examined against their outcomes, so the Company learns;
- **Responsible** — never at the cost of the Company's principles or its people;
- **Recorded** — significant investments and their reasoning preserved in the Company's memory.

---

## 46.5 Capital and Constancy

The Company's allocation of capital shall remain true to its principles while its circumstances evolve.

The Company's investments may change with its needs and its generations — but the standard by which they are judged shall not: capital shall always be allocated in service of the Company's purpose, for the long-term benefit of the Company and all who depend upon it.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **The Company's capital is allocated in service of its purpose — invested for long-term benefit, weighed with judgement, and never at the cost of the Company's principles.**
