# Chapter 34 — Assurance & Audit

*This chapter explains how verification, auditing and continual assessment strengthen trust. The Company's word is backed by evidence — and its evidence is examined, so that the Company's standards are real and provable.*

---

## 34.1 Assurance Through Verification

The Company's trust is strengthened by verification.

The Company does not ask others to take its word alone. It verifies its own work — checking that standards were met, testing what it produces, and examining how it operates — so that its claims are supported by evidence that can be relied upon.

Assurance is how the Company's principles become provable: the Company says what it does, does what it says, and can show it.

---

## 34.2 Auditing

The Company shall audit its own operations and be open to audit by others.

Auditing is the independent examination of the Company's systems and records — verifying that the Company's work meets its standards, its evidence is trustworthy and its claims are true.

The Company shall:

- conduct internal audits of its operations, quality systems and records;
- welcome external audits by certification bodies, regulators and customers;
- treat audit findings honestly — correcting what is found, not concealing it;
- learn from audits, using their findings to strengthen the Company.

---

## 34.3 Continual Assessment

The Company's assurance shall be continual, not occasional.

Standards shall be assessed regularly, processes examined as they are performed, and results reviewed as they are produced. Continual assessment catches drift before it becomes failure, and improvement before it is needed.

---

## 34.4 The Attitude to Findings

The Company's attitude to audit findings shall be that of a Company that wants to know the truth.

A finding that reveals a weakness is an opportunity to strengthen the Company, not a threat to be resisted. The Company shall respond to findings with honesty, correct their causes and verify that the correction holds.

The Company would rather find its own weaknesses in an audit than have them found in its products by its customers.

---

## 34.5 Assurance and Trust

Assurance is how the Company earns the trust of those who rely upon it.

Customers, certification bodies, regulators, partners and future generations trust the Company not only because of what it says, but because its claims can be examined and verified. Assurance and audit are therefore not burdens upon the Company's work; they are the instruments of its trustworthiness.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **The Company's trust is built on verified evidence. It audits its own work, welcomes examination by others, and treats every finding as an opportunity to strengthen itself.**
