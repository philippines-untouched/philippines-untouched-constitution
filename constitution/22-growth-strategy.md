# Chapter 22 — Growth Strategy

*This chapter defines sustainable expansion — growth that strengthens the Company without compromising its principles, its quality or its stewardship.*

---

## 22.1 Growth Serves the Company

The Company seeks growth that serves its Purpose and strengthens its long-term endurance.

Growth is not an end in itself. The Company grows to create more value — more quality products, more meaningful employment, more knowledge, more opportunity, more strength for the generations that follow.

Growth that weakens the Company's quality, its principles or its future is not growth; it is depletion in the guise of expansion.

---

## 22.2 The Principles of Growth

The Company's growth shall be:

- **Sustainable** — growth the Company can maintain without exhausting its people, its land, its resources or its finances;
- **Deliberate** — growth chosen for its contribution to the Company's purpose, not pursued for its own sake;
- **Consistent** — growth that preserves the quality and standards that earned the Company its position;
- **Integrated** — growth that strengthens the whole system of the Company, not one part at the expense of others;
- **Stewardship-minded** — growth that leaves the Company, its land and its communities stronger, never weaker.

---

## 22.3 The Pace of Growth

The Company shall grow at a pace it can sustain with integrity.

The Company shall not expand faster than its standards can follow. Every new activity, market or facility shall meet the same standards of quality, evidence and responsibility as the Company's established work.

The Company accepts that patient growth is the price of enduring quality. It shall not sacrifice what it has built to grow more quickly.

---

## 22.4 Growth and the Company's Principles

No growth shall compromise the Company's principles.

The Company shall decline opportunities that would require it to lower its standards, weaken its evidence, exploit its people or deplete its land — whatever the size of the opportunity.

A Company that preserves its principles while declining to grow remains true to its purpose. A Company that grows by abandoning its principles ceases to be itself.

---

## 22.5 Growth and the Future

The Company's growth shall strengthen what it leaves to future generations.

Each stage of growth shall build capability — new skills, new knowledge, new relationships and new strength — so that the Company's expansion is also its succession: the Company grows not only in size but in the capacity to endure.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **The Company grows sustainably — expanding only as fast as its standards can follow, and never at the cost of its principles, its quality or its future.**
