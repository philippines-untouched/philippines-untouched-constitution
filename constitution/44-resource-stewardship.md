# Chapter 44 — Resource Stewardship

*This chapter defines responsible management of land, water, energy and materials. The resources the Company uses are not its own property to consume; they are entrusted to it, and shall be used in a manner that preserves them for the future.*

---

## 44.1 Resources Are Entrusted

The resources the Company uses — its land, water, energy and materials — are entrusted to it.

The Company's work depends upon these resources, and its duty is to use them responsibly: to meet the Company's needs without depleting what future generations depend upon. Responsible use is the foundation of the Company's endurance.

---

## 44.2 Land

The Company shall manage its land responsibly:

- protecting and improving soil health;
- preventing degradation and erosion;
- using land for purposes that sustain its long-term productivity;
- leaving the land more fertile than it was found.

---

## 44.3 Water

The Company shall manage water responsibly:

- using water carefully and efficiently;
- protecting waterways from pollution and depletion;
- safeguarding the water that communities and ecosystems depend upon;
- managing water as a shared resource, not a private convenience.

---

## 44.4 Energy

The Company shall manage energy responsibly:

- using energy efficiently, avoiding waste;
- preferring renewable sources where practical;
- reducing the Company's dependence on resources that cannot be renewed;
- matching energy use to the value it creates.

---

## 44.5 Materials

The Company shall manage materials responsibly:

- using materials efficiently, avoiding waste;
- preferring materials that can be renewed, reused or recovered;
- managing unavoidable waste responsibly;
- choosing materials consistent with the Company's quality and its principles.

---

## 44.6 The Discipline of Resource Use

The Company's use of resources shall be disciplined:

- **Efficient** — achieving the required result with the least necessary use;
- **Responsible** — using resources in a manner that preserves them;
- **Measured** — knowing what the Company uses and how it is used;
- **Improving** — reducing waste and improving efficiency continually;
- **Accountable** — able to show that its resources were used responsibly.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **The resources entrusted to the Company — land, water, energy and materials — shall be used responsibly, so that they remain available to the generations that follow.**
