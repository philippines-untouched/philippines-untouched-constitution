# Chapter 21 — Market Strategy

*This chapter explains how the Company intends to compete — through quality, trust and authenticity, in the premium segment of the market, rather than primarily on price.*

---

## 21.1 The Company's Market Position

The Company competes in the premium segment of the market.

The Company does not primarily compete on commodity pricing. It competes through the quality of its products, the trust it earns and the authenticity of its provenance — the things that make its products worth more, honestly earned.

---

## 21.2 The Markets the Company Serves

The Company's primary target market is international export markets.

The Company's primary sales channel is direct online sales, through which the Company presents its products and its story to customers wherever they are.

Additional channels may include premium retail, wholesale, export distributors, hospitality, hotels and resorts, restaurants, professional chefs and strategic commercial partnerships — each consistent with the Company's premium position and its standards.

---

## 21.3 How the Company Competes

The Company competes through:

- **Quality** — products that meet uncompromising standards, consistently;
- **Trust** — conduct and records that customers can rely upon;
- **Authenticity** — genuine Philippine provenance, honestly presented;
- **Transparency** — honest information about products, practices and provenance;
- **Craftsmanship** — the care and skill evident in the Company's products;
- **Responsible production** — products made with respect for people and nature.

The Company's market position is earned through these qualities. It is not claimed by promotion alone.

---

## 21.4 The Premium Segment

Serving the premium segment is a discipline, not a label.

The Company shall price its products honestly — reflecting the quality, care and integrity that produced them — and shall never present a product above its true quality, nor below the standard the Company's name promises.

The premium position shall be maintained through the quality of the products themselves, so that the price the Company asks is always justified by what the customer receives.

---

## 21.5 Competition and Standards

The Company's market strategy is consistent with its principle of excellence without comparison.

The Company strives to be the best it can be through its own standards. It competes by improving itself — its quality, its trust, its authenticity — not by diminishing others.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **The Company competes in the premium segment through quality, trust and authenticity — earned by its products and its standards, not claimed by its words.**
