# Chapter 4 — Core Values

*This chapter defines the enduring values expected of every person acting on behalf of the Company. Core Values are not aspirations; they are the standard of conduct by which the Company and its people are measured.*

---

## 4.1 The Core Values

The Company holds five Core Values. They are expected of every person acting on behalf of the Company — directors, officers, executives, employees, contractors, consultants and partners — in every activity and at every level.

### Integrity

We act honestly, in word and deed. We do what we say, record what we do, and stand behind our commitments. Integrity is not conditional on convenience, pressure or reward.

### Quality

We give our best to everything we produce and everything we do. Quality is not an act performed for inspection; it is the standard of our daily work.

### Stewardship

We treat what has been entrusted to us — the land, the Company, its resources and its reputation — as something to be preserved and strengthened for those who follow.

### Trust

We earn trust through consistent, honest and verifiable conduct. Trust is the foundation of every relationship the Company has — with customers, people, partners and communities — and it is protected by evidence, not by words.

### Excellence

We strive to be the best we can be, through our own standards and continual improvement. We measure ourselves against what we are capable of becoming, not against others.

---

## 4.2 What the Values Mean in Practice

The Core Values are expressed through daily conduct:

- **Integrity** — reporting accurately, honouring promises, and refusing to take shortcuts that compromise what is right;
- **Quality** — performing every task to the same standard, caring about the detail, and never knowingly passing on work that is below standard;
- **Stewardship** — using resources responsibly, caring for the land and equipment, and leaving every place and every task better than it was found;
- **Trust** — keeping records that can be verified, communicating honestly, and behaving so that others may rely on the Company without hesitation;
- **Excellence** — learning continuously, improving steadily, and taking pride in work done well.

---

## 4.3 Values Are Not Optional

The Core Values apply to every person acting on behalf of the Company, without exception.

No commercial objective, instruction or pressure justifies conduct contrary to the Core Values. When a conflict arises between a short-term goal and a Core Value, the Core Value prevails.

Leadership carries a particular responsibility to demonstrate the Core Values in action, for the values of the Company are learned by example before they are learned by instruction.

---

## 4.4 The Values and the Company

The Core Values are the character of the Company. They bind the Company's people to a common standard of conduct, whatever their role, and they endure regardless of changes in leadership, ownership, technology or markets.

A person who cannot uphold the Core Values cannot fully act on behalf of the Company. A decision that cannot be reconciled with the Core Values is not a decision the Company should make.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **The Core Values — Integrity, Quality, Stewardship, Trust, Excellence — are the standard of conduct for every person acting on behalf of the Company, without exception.**
