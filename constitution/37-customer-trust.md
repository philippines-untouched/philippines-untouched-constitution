# Chapter 37 — Customer Trust

*This chapter defines how trust is earned, maintained and protected. The Company's relationship with its customers rests on one foundation: trust — earned through honesty, kept through consistency, and protected as the Company's most valuable relationship.*

---

## 37.1 Trust Is the Foundation

The Company's relationship with its customers rests upon trust.

Customers choose the Company's products because they believe in them — in their quality, their safety, their authenticity and the honesty of their presentation. That belief is the foundation of the Company's existence in the market, and it shall be earned and protected accordingly.

---

## 37.2 How Trust Is Earned

The Company earns customer trust through:

- **Quality** — products that meet their promises, consistently;
- **Honesty** — truthful presentation of products, provenance and practices;
- **Evidence** — claims supported by records that can be examined;
- **Consistency** — the same standard, the same truth, every time;
- **Care** — genuine attention to the safety and wellbeing of customers;
- **Responsiveness** — honest and timely response when something goes wrong.

Trust is earned product by product, transaction by transaction, and promise by promise.

---

## 37.3 How Trust Is Maintained

The Company maintains customer trust by never giving it cause to be doubted.

The Company shall:

- keep its promises, in every product and every claim;
- maintain its standards, even when no one is watching;
- tell the truth about its products, including what they are not;
- keep its records honest, so that its claims can always be verified;
- serve its customers fairly, in every market and every transaction.

---

## 37.4 How Trust Is Protected

The Company protects customer trust by acting with integrity when it is tested.

When a problem occurs — a product issue, a mistake, a failure — the Company shall respond with honesty and care: acknowledging the problem, correcting it, informing those affected, and learning so that it does not recur.

The Company recognises that a customer's trust, once broken, is hard to restore. It shall therefore act to protect trust before, during and after every difficulty — treating the customer's trust as more valuable than any short-term gain.

---

## 37.5 Trust and the Company's Future

Customer trust is the Company's inheritance and its future.

Each generation of customers extends the trust that earlier generations earned. The Company shall serve its customers so that the trust it receives is preserved, strengthened and passed on — to the customers of today and the customers of generations to come.

---

## Foundational Principle

> **Customer trust is the foundation of the Company's existence — earned through quality and honesty, maintained through consistency, and protected above short-term gain.**
